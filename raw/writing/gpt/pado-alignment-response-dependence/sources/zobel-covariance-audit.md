# Zobel 1983 covariance audit

The original six-page PDF was rendered and visually inspected on 2026-11-05.

## Direct observations

- Printed p.393 / PDF p.5, Fig. 5 plots the \(\alpha_2,\alpha_4\) pairs with
  marginal error bars for the \(\alpha\)-induced \(^{67}\mathrm{Ga}\) data.
- The paragraph below Fig. 5 states that the statistical dependence of
  \(\alpha_k\) should produce error ellipses larger than the rectangles formed
  by the marginal error bars.
- The paper does not print the numerical row-by-row covariance, ellipse
  parameters, raw angular counts, or a response matrix.
- Printed p.394 / PDF p.6 states that angular-distribution and linear-polarization
  measurements used together for model-independent spin assignments and mixing
  ratios should be taken at the same energy and target; it also warns against
  adopting a single Gaussian substate population when feeding components compete.

## Consequence for the manuscript

The missing covariance is a direct source boundary, not merely a failure to find a
supplementary file. The article may use the quoted marginal errors and profile
\(\rho\) as a declared sensitivity contract, but it cannot call the result a
reproduction of the original joint fit or infer a unique experimental
likelihood-ratio significance.
