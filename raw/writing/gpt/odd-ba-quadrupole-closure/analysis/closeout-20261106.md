Three completed checks change what that revision needs to say.

1. **Exact fixed-charge control.** An independently constructed unpaired
   particle–rotor example fixes \(\gamma=20^\circ\), charge amplitude and
   inertias. Varying its field strength over the five prescribed points
   leaves \(q_2=1,q_3=0.5\), while the all-15/2 ratio changes from .322617 to
   1.026250. This supports the distinction between angular coupling and
   charge geometry. It does not establish unchanged geometry along the
   separate IBFM path.
2. **Independent isomer check.** For the tentative 137Ba 19/2 isomer in
   Vogt2017, the primary lowest-state pair gives 22.47 W.u. and a 1173.6-keV
   modeled photon, versus .46(3) W.u. at120.1keV. Across the seven existing
   operators and two declared parent candidates, all-open model E2 rates
   give conditional half-life upper bounds at most \(2.811\times10^{-4}\)ns,
   versus589(20)ns. The bound assumes the model energies, amplitudes and
   mapping. A near-strength alternate branch cannot justify deleting a
   dominant fast route. This is outside the direct initial-11/2 cubic loop,
   but limits broader spectroscopic validation. The measured B and half-life
   are one linked evidence group.
3. **Source-energy reconstruction.** All six displayed negative-parity
   series of135/137Ba in the archived Nomura2017 arXiv-v2 Fig.5(c) were
   recovered before comparison. Ordering agrees; maximum nonreference
   differences are1.896/3.128keV. The figure has no19/2 or higher same-spin
   states, and the author run card/wavefunctions/EM figures remain
   unavailable. This does not certify the isomer mapping or identify an
   error in the original authors' calculation.
