# Argument and terminology

This package studies how angular-momentum-channel redistribution changes
effective quadrupole-shape diagnostics in the lowest 11/2-negative states of
129–137Ba, within one explicit, source-parameterized IBFM family. Its contribution
must be the quantified nuclear-structure separation and its mechanism, not the
known algebra of quadrupole invariants or a claim to correct published figures.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| complete moment | Full charge-polynomial moment within the stated finite model space |
| restricted proxy | Rotationally invariant contraction restricted to whole J+2 multiplets |
| channel saturation | Fraction of quadratic strength retained within that selected spin |
| full coverage | Retained quadratic contribution divided by complete q2 |
| rigid-equivalent angle | Conditional acos coordinate, undefined outside its real domain |
| core-only control | Same odd-system state and charge calibration, eF set to zero |
| intervention | Specified Hamiltonian-term change, with no refit and no posterior interpretation |

The shortest evidence chain is: fixed-J isotope results; convergence within the
selected spin versus full coverage; signed cubic cancellation and coupling
controls; operator dependence and physical-scope checks.

The main text retains conclusion-changing operator dependence and any static
moment mismatch. Full input metadata, algebraic controls, software checks and
state lists belong in the data/code package or supplement. No unrelated
129Ba rotor fit, angular-distribution study or Nambu-continuation calculation
belongs in this manuscript.

The calibrated main case, coupling path, operator sensitivity, static moments,
conditional calibration alternatives and updated closest-precedent comparison
are complete at the declared scope. The later Vogt2017 experiment supplies a
tentative 137Ba target assignment but not its required absolute E2 denominator.
The manuscript keeps the primary static-moment overprediction, the spectral
cost of a Qs-only Hamiltonian match, and outside-domain complete ratios in
some alternative calibrations.

Internal scientific review and numerical replay are recorded in this directory.
Human authorship details remain placeholders. No submission, acceptance,
unique empirical shape mechanism or completed full-library reading is claimed.
