# `129Ba`

## Identity

Odd-neutron `Z=56`, `N=73` nucleus in the A≈130 region.

## Production Reactions

Legacy data discussed in the reanalysis come from `120Sn(12C,3nγ)129Ba` and `116Cd(18O,5nγ)129Ba`.

## Bands

[[chakraborty-2024-possibility-wobbling-129ba]] reinterprets legacy data: band 2 is favoured as the `νh11/2` signature partner, while band 3 is proposed as a likely first-phonon longitudinal-wobbling sequence. The source explicitly requires new measurements for unequivocal establishment.

## Competing Interpretations

Signature partner, longitudinal wobbling and TiP remain possible at different evidence levels.
