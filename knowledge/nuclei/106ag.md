# `106Ag`

## Identity

`Z=47, N=59` odd-odd nucleus used in [[bark-2024-investigations-nuclear-chirality-ithembalabs]] as a secondary counterexample to assigning chiral partnership from crossing/near-degenerate bands alone.

## Secondary Experimental Structure

The review summarizes three negative-parity bands. Band 1 is assigned `πg9/2^-1⊗νh11/2`; Bands 2 and 3 are assigned mixed four-quasiparticle `πg9/2^-1⊗ν(g7/2,d5/2)^2h11/2` structures. The two earlier putative partners cross near `I≈14ℏ`, but their aligned angular momenta differ by about `4ℏ` (BK24-15; PDF pp.22-24, Figs.30-32).

## Lifetime and Model Evidence

The summarized AFRODITE DSAM lifetimes have typical uncertainty near 20%. Together with branching ratios they yield absolute `B(M1)`/`B(E2)` values. Empirical alignment additivity, RMF deformation/configuration ordering and TPRM energy/transition comparisons support the two- versus four-quasiparticle reassignment rather than a simple chiral pair (BK24-15).

## Production Reactions

The review lists `90Zr(14N,4n)` at `71 MeV` with thin and thick target runs. This is literal secondary Table 1 metadata, with an unresolved mass-number boundary: 90+14−4=100, not 106. Rather RA14-2 directly reports 96Zr at 68 MeV in a different acquisition. Do not silently replace the review target or infer the Lieder target from Rather (RA14-11/12).
