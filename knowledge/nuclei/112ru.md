# `112Ru`

## Identity

`Z=44, N=68` even-even nucleus. Luo 2009 proposes a soft chiral-vibration doublet but emphasizes that the observed band crossing enters a chiral-instability regime beyond harmonic RPA.

## Production Reactions

- Prompt product of `252Cf` spontaneous fission: [[gammasphere-cf252-fission-ru108-112]].

## Established Level Structure

Negative-parity composite sequences Bands 4-5 and 6-7 remain close and cross. They show similar branching-derived ratios and comparable constant `S(I)`.

## Bands

- [[112ru-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]]: zero-/one-phonon assignment with an explicit crossing/RPA-breakdown boundary.

## Deformation and Collective Features

Prior positive-parity systematics and IBM variants favor triaxial rigidity. The paper transfers the TAC/RPA interpretation from nearby `110Ru`, but states direct harmonic comparison is inappropriate at the crossing.

## Competing Interpretations

The crossing can signal chiral instability or more complicated slow orientation motion. A large-amplitude description is required; static chirality is not obtained in the planar TAC solution.

## Open Questions

- What large-amplitude collective coordinate describes the crossing?
- Do absolute partner strengths and mixing ratios survive the crossing?
