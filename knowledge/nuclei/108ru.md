# `108Ru`

## Identity

`Z=44, N=64` even-even nucleus. Luo 2009 identifies negative-parity `ΔI=1` doublet structures but treats this γ-soft nucleus as a control rather than a retained chiral-vibration case.

## Production Reactions

- Prompt product of `252Cf` spontaneous fission: [[gammasphere-cf252-fission-ru108-112]].

## Established Level Structure

Figure 1 presents new negative-parity composite sequences 4-5 and 6-7. Selected γ-γ angular correlations support spins/dipole transitions, and reported bandhead lifetimes below `1 ns` are used to reject high-K bands.

## Bands

- [[108ru-negative-parity-doublet-structures]]: two composite `ΔI=1` structures with different signature-staggering behavior.

## Deformation and Collective Features

Earlier positive-parity γ-band/IBM comparisons classify `108Ru` as γ soft. That classification is external model/systematics context, not a direct shape measurement in Luo 2009.

## Competing Interpretations

The two negative-parity structures may belong to different configurations. The authors prefer γ softness disturbing the doublets by analogy with `106Ag`, but do not continue a quantitative chiral analysis.

## Open Questions

- Do absolute strengths and configuration-sensitive observables support one common structure?
- Can a γ-soft large-amplitude calculation reproduce the mismatched signature patterns?
