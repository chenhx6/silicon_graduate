# Comprehensive analysis of the scissors mode in heavy even-even nuclei

## Bibliographic Record

PRC 59, R1851-R1854 (1999), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevC.59.R1851`.

## Scope and Reading Depth

- Completed reading_depth: `deep-read`
- Covered scope: Global systematic analysis of Nuclear Resonance Fluorescence (NRF) measurements for the scissors mode across 35 deformed rare-earth and actinide even-even nuclei; extraction of summed $B(M1)\uparrow$ strength; comparison with macroscopic Two-Rotor Model (TRM) and microscopic QRPA sum rules; identification and formalization of the $\beta_2$ collectivity saturation phenomenon at $\beta_2 \ge 0.25$.
- Not covered: Individual high-energy fragmentation states beyond the scissors resonance centroid.
- Coverage caveats: Focuses on the total integrated orbital $M1$ strength in the $2.5 - 3.5\text{ MeV}$ window.

## Cross-Mass-Region Transferability Significance

1. **宏观流体两转子标度向超形变平移的致命缺陷**：证明不能简单把低能剪刀模的 $\beta_2^2$ 标度外推至超形变区；微观配对收缩与价空间饱和构成了宏观两流体模型的**不适用性硬边界**。
2. **稀土区向锕系区的普适价空间归一**：解释了为何质量数大幅增加的锕系核（$^{238}\text{U}$）的剪刀模强度（$3.4\ \mu_N^2$）并未显著超越稀土核（$^{156}\text{Gd}$ 的 $3.1\ \mu_N^2$），确立了价核子数控制的饱和顶棚。
