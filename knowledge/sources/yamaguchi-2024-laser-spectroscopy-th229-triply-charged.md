## Summary

Yamaguchi et al. trapped 229mTh3+ ions supplied continuously by a 233U alpha-decay source in a linear Paul trap. Nuclear-state-selective laser spectroscopy determined the nuclear decay half-life of isolated 229mTh3+ in vacuum to be T1/2 = 1400 (+600/-300) s (tau ≈ 2000 s), establishing the natural linewidth Delta_nu_0 ≈ 80 μHz and natural Q ≈ 2.5 x 10^19. Hyperfine constants A and B were also measured.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

Demonstrates spectroscopy and lifetime of 229mTh3+ in an ion trap; closed-loop clock stabilization on this single-ion transition was not performed in this work.
