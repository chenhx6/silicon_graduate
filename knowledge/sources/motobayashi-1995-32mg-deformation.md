# Large deformation of the very neutron-rich nucleus `32Mg` from intermediate-energy Coulomb excitation

## Bibliographic Record

PLB 346, 9-14 (1995), DOI `10.1016/0370-2693(95)00012-A`.

## Scope and Reading Depth

- Completed reading_depth: `deep-read`
- Covered scope: Intermediate-energy Coulomb excitation of a radioactive beam of $^{32}\text{Mg}$ at 49.2 MeV/nucleon on a $^{208}\text{Pb}$ target at the RIKEN Ring Cyclotron Facility; measurement of de-excitation $\gamma$ rays with a 68-detector NaI(Tl) array; extraction of the $B(E2; 0^+ \to 2^+_1)$ transition probability and ground-state deformation parameter $\beta_2$; discovery of large prolate deformation ($\beta_2 \approx 0.51$) at $N=20$; proof of the breakdown of the $N=20$ spherical shell closure in magnesium isotopes.
- Not covered: Fusion-evaporation high-spin spectroscopy.
- Coverage caveats: Focuses on the ground-state $0^+ \to 2^+_1$ collectivity.

## Cross-Mass-Region Transferability Significance

1. **经典幻数跨同位旋迁移的彻底破缺**：确立了 $N=20$ 绝非核图上不可改变的永恒常数；单极张量力驱动的壳层演化会在中子过剩区使球形幻数彻底崩溃为强长椭形变区。
2. **壳模型单粒子基底适用性边界**：证明将稳定核 $0\hbar\omega$ 壳模型空间直接平移至反转岛核区会严重低估四极集体性达 $600\%$，必须强制引入包含高阶 $2\hbar\omega$ 侵入组态的多主壳模型空间。
