# Measurement of the linear polarization of continuum gamma rays from (32S, xn) reactions to prolate and oblate rare-earth nuclei

## Bibliographic Record

W. Trautmann et al., Nuclear Physics A 378, 141–158 (1982), complete publisher version.

## Scope and Reading Depth

PDF 1–18（印 141–158），§§1–4、校准、连续谱分析及引用。

## Summary

用三只 Ge(Li) Compton 极化计测稀土核连续谱，并以校准的灵敏度和角分布约束多极成分。

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- Pγ 和 A₂ 的谱成分分解含对齐度和参考极化输入。该结果约束连续谱中多极成分，未给离散混合振幅。
