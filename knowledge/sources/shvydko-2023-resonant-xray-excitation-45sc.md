## Summary

Shvyd'ko et al. reported resonant hard X-ray excitation of the 45Sc nuclear clock isomer at 12.39 keV using European XFEL pulses. In metallic scandium foil, the transition energy was determined as E0 = 12,389.59 ± 0.15 (stat) + 0.12 (syst) eV (lambda0 = 1.00071 Å), with natural lifetime tau0 = 470(6) ms, natural linewidth Gamma0 = 1.40(2) feV (Delta_nu_0 ≈ 0.338 Hz), natural Q = 8.85(10) x 10^18, and room-temperature Mössbauer recoilless fraction f_LM ≈ 0.75.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

Transition energy is in the hard X-ray band (12.4 keV), requiring large accelerator infrastructure (European XFEL) rather than tabletop lasers. This study does not establish the same model-dependent fundamental-constant sensitivity as discussed for 229Th; no sensitivity factor is inferred from transition energy alone.
