by D. G. Roux (PDF p. 1). The institutional repository supplied a readable

## Evidence from the abstract

- The author reports populating high-spin states in 194Tl with
  181Ta(18O,5n) at 91 and 93 MeV, using AFRODITE at iThemba LABS
  (PDF p. 3, abstract; author-reported experiment).
- The abstract describes linear polarization and recoil shadow anisotropy
  lifetime measurements, and says polarization anisotropies were used for
  parity assignments (PDF p. 3; author-reported analysis).
