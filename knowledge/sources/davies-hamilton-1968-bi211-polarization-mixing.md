# Mixing-ratio intervals from shared \(^{211}\mathrm{Bi}\) cascades

## Bibliographic Record

K. E. Davies and W. D. Hamilton, *Nuclear Physics A* 114, 589–601 (1968). DOI `10.1016/0375-9474(68)90287-X`.

## Scope and Reading Depth

Read all 13 publisher pages, including Table 1, Figs. 1–9, apparatus, solution construction and comparison with conversion data and the shell model. PDF p.1 corresponds to printed p.589.

## Summary

Angular correlations of two cascades in \(^{211}\mathrm{Bi}\), populated by \(^{211}\mathrm{Pb}\) decay, share the 405 keV second transition. A NaI Compton polarimeter cannot resolve the 405 and 427 keV rays. At a relative opening angle of \(90^\circ\), it measures the weighted polarization correlation from analyzing either member of that cascade. The published \(k=N_0/N_{90}=0.941\pm0.036\) yields \(P=+0.066\pm0.040\) for the unresolved pair under the authors' calibration, with sensitivity \(0.46\pm0.04\) at 415 keV. The experiment corrects counting losses, random coincidences and source miscentering. Printed pp.592–594, Sec. 4 and Figs. 2–3.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- The angular and polarization contours use experimental upper and lower limits. The paper treats the transformed allowed regions as band constraints. It obtains the reported intervals from the correlation bands for the selected spin sequence. The prior internal-conversion evidence selects the family consistent with all measurements from the surviving candidates in Fig. 8. These roles are distinguished in pp.597–600, including the text immediately above Fig. 7.
- The two relative-angle coefficient sets constrain a common \(\delta(405)\); each may narrow its range, but the shared transition provides only one physical mixing ratio. The \(P\) measured for 427–405 is not an independent \(P(405)\), \(P(427)\) or \(P(702)\). The method reports no Gaussian in-beam \(\sigma/I\) because it is a radioactive-decay cascade correlation.
- Use 702 keV in the dataset, matching the abstract, Table 1 column, figures and final interval. Table 1's caption gives 700 keV and the final discussion on p.601 gives 720 keV. These are retained as source-label discrepancies. Fig. 8 also prints 427 keV in its level heading where Fig. 9 places the first level at 405 keV.
- The shell-model discussion distinguishes qualitative spin agreement from unresolved energies, lifetimes and strong E2 admixtures. The correlations constrain the decay scheme under the assumed ground-state spin and tested spin range. Printed pp.599–601, Sec. 6.
