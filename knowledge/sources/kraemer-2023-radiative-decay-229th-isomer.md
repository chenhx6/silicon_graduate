## Summary

ISOLDE 经 ²²⁹Ac β 馈入，在 MgF₂ 和 CaF₂ 观测约 148.7 nm 的辐射。小狭缝组合给 148.71±0.06(stat.)±0.41(syst.) nm、8.338±0.003(stat.)±0.023(syst.) eV。MgF₂ 中报告半衰期 670(102) s。

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

Decay observed in solid crystals; crystal local field effects (macroscopic n^3 approximation) modify the decay rate relative to vacuum. Non-radiative quenching by lattice defects cannot be entirely ruled out.
