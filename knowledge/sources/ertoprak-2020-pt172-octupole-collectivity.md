## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- Same second acquisition reused. Cederwall et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 121, 022502 (2018), Ref. [29]. PDF 2–3, §2 and p. 9 Ref. [29].
- Earlier level scheme. Joss et al., Phys. Rev. C 74, 014302 (2006), Ref. [38]. PDF 4 and p. 9 Ref. [38].
- Reference multipolarities in competing channel. Bark et al., Nucl. Phys. A 657, 113 (1999), Ref. [39]. PDF 4 and p. 7 Fig. 4.
- Fig. 4 publishes scatter asymmetry A and angular ratio R_A, with multipolarity labels. No numerical photon P plus δ pair.
- B(E3)≈48(13) W.u. is an estimate based on neighboring-nucleus E1 strengths. A direct 3⁻ lifetime is absent. Octupole collectivity does not establish a static deformation.
- Measured Compton asymmetry A, not an extracted degree. No signed δ.
