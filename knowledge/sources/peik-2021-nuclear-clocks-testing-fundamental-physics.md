## Summary

Peik et al. published a 38-page comprehensive review in Quantum Science and Technology on testing fundamental physics with nuclear clocks. They derived theoretical sensitivity coefficients for variations in alpha (K_alpha ≈ 10^4) and quark mass (K_q ≈ -10^4), analyzed ultralight dark matter searches (oscillatory and transient signals), and compared clock architectures.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

Sensitivity coefficients K_alpha and K_q are theoretical model estimates with literature values spanning 10^3 to 10^5 across different nuclear models, not experimentally measured universal constants.
