# Systematics of low-lying dipole strengths in odd and even Dy and Gd isotopes

## Bibliographic Record

PRC 52, 2429-2443 (1995), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevC.52.2429`.

## Scope and Reading Depth

- Completed reading_depth: `deep-read`
- Covered scope: Systematic Nuclear Resonance Fluorescence (NRF) measurements using continuous bremsstrahlung up to 4.1 MeV on odd-mass ($^{155}\text{Gd}, ^{157}\text{Gd}, ^{161}\text{Dy}, ^{163}\text{Dy}$) and neighboring even-even ($^{156}\text{Gd}, ^{158}\text{Gd}, ^{162}\text{Dy}, ^{164}\text{Dy}$) isotopes; extraction of individual and integrated $B(M1)\uparrow$ dipole transition strengths; comparison of fragmentation patterns and centroid energies; analysis of Pauli blocking by the unpaired valence neutron.
- Not covered: Charged particle transfer reaction channels.
- Coverage caveats: Focuses on the $2.5 - 3.5\text{ MeV}$ scissors excitation window.

## Cross-Mass-Region Transferability Significance

1. **偶偶核集体模向奇 $A$ 核迁移的破缺**：确立了不能简单将偶偶核的集中型剪刀模参数直接平移至奇 $A$ 核；单奇核子诱发的泡利阻塞与态密度雪崩是导致集体模碎裂与表观强度损失的**通用机制边界**。
2. **微观态密度与可探测性阈值效应**：澄清了奇 $A$ 核中“缺失的剪刀强度”并非真正消失，而是由于微观碎裂至亚阈值细线（Sub-threshold Lines），形式化了核探测极限与多体耗散的边界。
