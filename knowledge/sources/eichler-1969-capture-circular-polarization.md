## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- Apparatus precursor. Eichler, Z. Physik 213, 352 (1968), Ref. [3]. PDF 2 end of §2 discussion/footnote.
- Experimental calibration input. S(n,γ) count calibration and estimated target depolarization. PDF 2–4, Eqs. (3)–(4), Table 1.
- Abstract and German body disagree on the ²⁸Al δ central estimate and notation. Preserve each original locator.
- Table 1 and Fig. 1 use 7.73 MeV; p. 4 calls the branch 7.5 MeV. The Al and Sm runs required a separate beam/polarimeter calibration.
- Circular polarization relation P_c=P_n R cos Θ; table gives corrected R. No linear P+δ pair.
