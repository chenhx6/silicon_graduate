# Low-temperature orientation and the 935 keV \(^{55}\mathrm{Fe}\) mixing ratio

## Bibliographic Record

R. W. Bauer and M. Deutsch, *Nuclear Physics* 16, 264–277 (1960). DOI `10.1016/S0029-5582(60)81036-X`.

## Scope and Reading Depth

Read the 14 publisher pages, including the alternative decay schemes, tensor formalism, cryogenic orientation and Compton polarimeter, Tables 1–2, Figs. 1–10 and references. PDF p.1 corresponds to print p.264.

## Summary

The source orients \(^{55}\mathrm{Co}\) in cerium-magnesium nitrate and analyzes γ rays in the daughter \(^{55}\mathrm{Fe}\). Fig. 10 shows the simultaneous warm-up curves of angular anisotropy \(\epsilon=[W(90^\circ)-W(0)]/W(90^\circ)\) and the measured polarization effect \(P_LQ_L=(R-1)/(R+1)\), with \(R=N_\perp/N_\parallel\). The 935 keV and 1.41 MeV signals have opposite polarization signs. \(P_L\) values in Tables 1–2 are predictions at specified angular anisotropies, whereas the plotted \(P_LQ_L\) is measured. Printed pp.266–267, 271–276, Eqs. (3),(6),(7), Tables 1–2 and Fig. 10.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- The 1.41 MeV line serves as an orientation reference under the pure-E2 hypothesis in Table 1. The competing sequence instead treats the 935 keV ray as pure E2. Polarimetry tests the spin/multipole alternatives after computing the precursor-dependent deorientation factors. The β Fermi/Gamow–Teller admixture is a separate uncertainty. The angular ratio here refers to the low-temperature orientation axis and incorporates intermediate-decay assumptions.
- The second-member convention of Biedenharn and Rose is explicit in Eq. (7) and both table captions. Compare signs only after mapping the operator convention and cascade position. The large \(+15\pm6\) solution is an angular candidate eliminated by the polarization comparison.
- The abstract mentions \(4.0\pm0.4\) nuclear magnetons for a pure Fermi 1.03 MeV β branch, whereas Table 1 and the final discussion give \(4.60\pm0.40\) for that limit in the selected sequence. The \(5.3\pm0.4\) mainly Gamow–Teller result is common to the abstract and the selected discussion. The magnetic-moment alternatives are conditional on the β input.
