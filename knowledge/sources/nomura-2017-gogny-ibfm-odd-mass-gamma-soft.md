# Shape transitions in odd-mass γ-soft nuclei within the interacting boson-fermion model based on the Gogny energy density functional

## Bibliographic Record

Nomura et al., arXiv:1710.03908v2. Local manifest associates PRC 96, 064316, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevC.96.064316.

## Scope and Reading Depth

## Summary

Gogny-D1M 约束 HFB 与偶芯势能面给定 IBFM 输入，粒子耦合按核与宇称拟合谱。球形单粒子能和占据的 HFB 输入以奇数粒子数约束偶数宇称真空。
