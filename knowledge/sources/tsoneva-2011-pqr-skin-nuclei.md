# Pygmy Quadrupole Resonance in skin nuclei

## Bibliographic Record

PLB 695, 174-180 (2011), DOI `10.1016/j.physletb.2010.11.002`.

## Scope and Reading Depth

- Completed reading_depth: `deep-read`
- Covered scope: Microscopic investigation of low-lying $E2$ quadrupole response in stable and exotic neutron-rich nuclei using the Quasiparticle-Phonon Nuclear Model (QPNM) and self-consistent QRPA; systematic calculation of radial transition densities $\delta\rho_p(r)$ and $\delta\rho_n(r)$; discovery of the Pygmy Quadrupole Resonance (PQR) in the energy region $E_x \sim 10 - 13\text{ MeV}$; comparison of the PQR with the standard ISGQR and IVGQR; analysis of valence neutron skin decoupling along the nickel ($^{58}\text{Ni} \to ^{78}\text{Ni}$) and tin ($^{112}\text{Sn} \to ^{132}\text{Sn}$) isotopic chains.
- Not covered: Fusion evaporation cross sections.
- Coverage caveats: Focuses on the structural separation between skin and core oscillations.

## Cross-Mass-Region Transferability Significance

1. **巨共振同位旋对称流向中子皮退耦的转变**：确立了稳定核的“质子-中子全核同相/反相纯多极流动”在存在致密中子皮时发生解耦，出现类似“矮模式”的第二多极相。
2. **朗道连续谱耗散对高阶巨共振的溶解**：阐明在富中子滴线区，深连续谱耦合必然导致高阶同位旋巨共振（IVGQR, IVGMR）融化为平坦本底，破除了在滴线区寻找尖锐高能巨共振的错误假设。
