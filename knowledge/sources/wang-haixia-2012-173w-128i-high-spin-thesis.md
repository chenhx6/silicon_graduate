## Paper Question and Scientific Motivation

- Author-explicit motivation: extend `173W` bands to high spin to test `ν5/2−[512]` signature inversion and its relation to `174Re`; determine whether the N=75 odd-odd `128I` scheme retains collectivity near N=82 (PDF pp.1–2, 56–58, 75–76).

## Method and Design Logic

- `173W`: 135/140-MeV `28Si` on `150Nd`, 14 BGO-suppressed HPGe, `1.7×10^8` γ-γ events; ADO geometry uses ≈40°/140° versus 90° (PDF pp.58–59).
- `128I`: 28/32-MeV `7Li` on `124Sn`, 9 BGO-suppressed HPGe plus LEPS; `67×10^6` γ-γ-t and `3×10^6` X-γ events; the two beam energies separate `128I` from `127I` lines (PDF pp.75–76).
- Schemes use coincidence, energy sums, intensity balance, ADO and known low-lying anchors; `173W` ratios use branching and an assumed `δ²=0` upper-bound convention (PDF pp.59–69).

## Key Evidence and Reasoning Chain

1. `173W` Bands 1–3 are extended: `ν7/2+[633]` to `(49/2)+`, `ν5/2−[512]` to `(47/2)−`, and `ν1/2−[521]` with its unfavored branch to `(49/2)−` (PDF pp.59–64, 90).
2. Band 2 shows a crossing near `ħω≈0.26 MeV`, gain ≈`7ħ`, and a post-crossing signature inversion; N=99 isotones show a regular inversion-point trend (PDF pp.69–71, 90).
3. `B(M1)/B(E2)` data agree better with pure `ν5/2−[512](f7/2)` than with `ν5/2−[523](h9/2)`, so the thesis argues against strong configuration mixing despite PRM calculations that had invoked it (PDF pp.71–73).
4. `128I` is rebuilt to 48 levels and 72 γ transitions up to `Iπ=16−`; the 102-keV triplet and 411-keV doublet are separated by beam-energy dependence and gated spectra (PDF pp.75–81).
5. Irregular spacings and parallel branches lead the author to a predominantly two-/multi-quasiparticle interpretation, with `πd5/2/g7/2⊗νh11/2` and `πh11/2⊗νh11/2` candidates (PDF pp.78–82).

## Summary

The thesis gives a high-spin `173W` signature-inversion case and a revised `128I` near-spherical level scheme. The `173W` inversion is experimentally established at the band-systematics level but its microscopic cause is unresolved; the `128I` result supports weakened collectivity and increasing single-particle character near N=82. Chapter 7’s detector-development work is outside this source page’s collective-motion scope.

## Nuclear Structure Information

- `173W`: three rotational bands; Band 2 is the `ν5/2−[512]` inversion case; Band 3 is a `ν1/2−[521]` decoupled band with a proposed `1.31(15) μs` band-head lifetime estimate from intensity balance (PDF p.64).
- `128I`: 6− isomer at 167.4 keV anchors the scheme; 183.0, 285.2, 696.2 keV negative-parity states are discussed as `g7/2/d5/2⊗h11/2` multiplet candidates; 1461/1562 keV are proposed `h11/2⊗h11/2` members.

## Authors' Interpretation

The author treats `173W` as a deformed collective-rotation case with single-particle signature effects and `128I` as a near-spherical, single-particle-dominated case. The `173W` inversion is explicitly not resolved by the available model comparison.

## Model Results

- CSM/Harris alignment and Routhian analyses support the band comparisons.
- PRM calculations reproduce some `173W` ratios but their proposed `ν5/2−[512]/ν5/2−[523]` mixing is in tension with the empirical ratio systematics (PDF pp.71–73).
- Empirical shell-model/systematics arguments place `128I` multiplet candidates; these are model-assisted assignments, not direct orbital measurements.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- ADO gives multipolarity class but cannot generally separate ΔI=2 from ΔI=0; the `128I` spin assignments therefore retain uncertainty (PDF pp.78–79).
- `173W` inversion may involve configuration mixing, triaxiality, residual interactions or other mechanisms; the thesis does not select one uniquely.
- `128I` configuration candidates are inferred from systematics and transfer/decay references; the thesis does not provide lifetimes or complete electromagnetic strengths for these high-spin states.
