## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- Separate acceptance calibration. ¹³⁷Cs standard-source run in this paper. PDF 1 and 3, Fig. 2, Eq. (2).
- Model-generated polarization input. GOSIA tensors, Ref. [3]. PDF 2 below Eq. (1).
- The predicted P from GOSIA and measured azimuthal asymmetry yield analyzing power. Fig. 3 gives 555.8 keV for ¹⁰⁴Pd; p. 1 says 558 keV.
- The ²²Na analysis was still in progress.
- Measured azimuthal asymmetry divided by predicted incident P. No signed δ.
