## Summary

Seiferle et al. measured the transition energy of 229mTh directly by internal conversion electron spectroscopy using an electrostatic retarding field magnetic bottle spectrometer. They determined the excitation energy to be E = 8.28 ± 0.17 eV (lambda = 149.7 ± 3.1 nm), ruling out earlier 7.6-7.8 eV estimates and defining the VUV laser search window.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

Relies on calibration of contact potential differences and work functions on metal/graphite catcher surfaces. Uncertainty of 0.17 eV required further narrowing by optical methods.
