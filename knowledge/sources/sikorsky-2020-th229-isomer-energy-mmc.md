## Summary

Sikorsky et al. measured the 0-60 keV gamma-ray spectrum of 233U decay using a cryogenic magnetic microcalorimeter (maXs-30 at 20 mK) with ~10 eV FWHM resolution. Evaluating four complementary decay schemes yielded an isomer energy of E = 8.10 ± 0.17 eV (lambda = 153.1 ± 3.2 nm), establishing the 8.10-8.35 eV global envelope.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

Center value (8.10 eV) is ~0.2 eV lower than Seiferle (8.28 eV) and Yamaguchi (8.30 eV), though consistent within 1-sigma combined uncertainties. Dependent on calibration gain linearity across 0-60 keV.
