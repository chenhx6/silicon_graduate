## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- Preliminary report of this work. Dhal et al., Ref. [9], DAE-BRNS symposium proceedings. PDF 2 preceding §2; PDF 9 Ref. [9].
- External calibration. Ref. [23] Clover sensitivity. PDF 2, §2.
- Table 1 and Fig. 3 show detector asymmetry ΔIPDCO. The paper gives a conversion P=Δ/Q and takes Q from a similar Clover setup, but provides no transition-level signed δ and calibrated P pair.
- The proposed E1/M2 links have low precision and rely on the decay assignments. The γ values are model minima, not direct shape measurements.
- Measured ΔIPDCO with a P conversion described. No sourced numeric P+δ pair.
