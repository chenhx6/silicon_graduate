## Key Results

- The `114Cd(48Ca,6nγ)` reaction at 215 MeV was measured with 101 Gammasphere HPGe detectors. Four weak bands (2, 7, 8, 9) were established in `156Er`; bands 2 and 7 reach `14+` and `(22+)`, while band 8 contains odd- and even-spin members of the γ-vibrational structure (PDF pp.1–3, Fig.2).
- The DCO-like ratio `R=Iγγ[θ≈130°(50°)]/Iγγ[θ≈90°]` is about 0.7 for stretched dipoles and 1.1 for stretched quadrupoles in this setup. The 1038-keV link has `R=0.55(9)` and supports the odd-spin assignment in band 8; the 237-keV band-9 transition has `R=1.31(24)` but a nonstretched-dipole alternative is retained (PDF pp.2–4, Table I).
- The 365-keV band-9 link has `R=0.27(3)`, too small for a pure dipole; the authors favor a large-negative-δ M1/E2 interpretation, then use the 509-keV E1 link to fix the 2997-keV state as `10+` and the band head as `8+` (PDF pp.3–4, Table I).
- The second `0+` state and `2+γ` head are nearly degenerate at 930 keV. The γ-band staggering persists to `I=16`; `S(4)` is close to −1, matching the Wilets–Jean γ-soft limit and differing from the Davydov–Filippov rigid-γ expectation (PDF pp.4–6, Figs.4–8).
- Alignment plots show the usual `(νi13/2)2` gain in bands 1, 2 and 7, while band 9 gains only about `7.5ℏ` above `ω≈0.3 MeV/ℏ`; the authors attribute band 9 to a competing aligned `(νh9/2,f7/2)2` configuration, with shape-change/termination alternatives retained from the companion high-spin study (PDF pp.6–7, Figs.9–10).

## Summary

Rees *et al.* add weak, non-yrast structures that connect low-energy γ-soft diagnostics to high-spin alignment systematics in `156Er`. The direct level and coincidence evidence is strong for the new bands, whereas γ-softness and the band-9 configuration remain interpretations that depend on model comparisons and competing branches.

## Competing Interpretations and Limitations

- `S(4)` is a useful γ-band diagnostic but is not a unique microscopic shape observable; the paper compares γ-soft and rigid-γ limits and also notes vibrational/phase-transition systematics.
- The angular-intensity ratio uses a specific Gammasphere geometry and reference transitions. It supports, but does not alone prove, each spin/multipolarity assignment.
- The `(νh9/2,f7/2)2` assignment for band 9 is inferred from its reduced alignment relative to the `(νi13/2)2` pattern and neighboring nuclei; configuration mixing and the companion high-spin band-termination analysis remain alternatives.
- The 930-keV second-`0+` interpretation is compatible with β vibration, pairing isomer and particle-hole/vacuum alternatives discussed in the paper; the source does not resolve them by itself.
