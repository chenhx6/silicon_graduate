# Nuclear Chirality and Multiple Chiral Doublet Bands

## Active Summary for Agents

本页是 evidence map / writing-support project，不是论文草稿。Meng 2010、Ayangeakaa 2013 与 Liu 2016 均已 deep-read 并完成人工 source review；本 project 页仍保留独立 review 状态。所有解释必须与 observed facts、experimental assignments、author interpretations 和 model calculations 分层。

Guo 2024 adds an unreviewed `74Br` chiral-wobbler candidate with ADO/polarization, DSAM lifetimes, transition probabilities and PRM. It extends the scope but does not alter the reviewed `133Ce/78Br` source claims.

Mukhopadhyay 2008 provides a direct `136Nd` counterexample to energy-degeneracy-only reading: DSAM-derived `B(E2)` values of the two near-degenerate negative-parity bands differ by factors of about 2–3, and TAC+RPA favors distinct configurations with band mixing. Petrache 2006 generalizes the warning to `134Pr/136Pm`: crossings can compress the level spacing, while the extracted `Q0,1/Q0,2=2.0(4)` and alignment differences contradict an ideal chiral-pair assumption. These sources raise the project's minimum test from “near-degeneracy + links” to partner-resolved electromagnetic strengths, alignment and crossing alternatives.

HS-126 is an alternate PDF of the Guo 2024 PRL. Its title, DOI, figures, tables, equations and conclusions agree with the canonical copy; it is audited version evidence, not a new experiment.

Frauendorf 2001 is a reviewed review/background source for the rotating-mean-field symmetry map. It supplies PAC/planar/aplanar TAC distinctions, the TAC/PRM and symmetry-restoration boundary, and comparison mechanisms such as magnetic, antimagnetic and reflection-asymmetric rotation. It is not an additional original experiment and does not change the reviewed status of the Meng/Ayangeakaa/Liu claims.

Petrache 1996 is an unreviewed historical precursor for `134Pr`: it establishes Bands 1-4 and the unresolved Band 1/Band 2 interpretation, but predates nuclear-chirality terminology. Its role here is retrospective and explicitly not direct chiral evidence.

Petrache 1998 is an unreviewed lifetime/deformation control source for `134,135Nd` and `131Ce`. It measures highly deformed bands but neither uses chirality nor crosswalks its `135Nd` HD band to later chiral doublets; it must not be counted as direct chiral lifetime evidence.

Klemme 1999 is an unreviewed precision-lifetime/method precursor. It adds absolute lifetimes to the low-spin `135Nd [514]9/2` sequence later called D1 and one `134Pr 9+` state, but studies no partner-band geometry or chiral doublet.

Hecht 2001 is the first direct chiral-candidate experiment in the rolling corpus. It adds linked positive-parity yrast/new-band pairs in `136Pm` and `138Eu`, DCO plus selected `136Pm` polarization, common-configuration systematics and 3D TAC. Its title says symmetry breaking, but its calibrated conclusion is “good candidates”; TAC does not calculate the quantum doublet mixing.

Starosta 2001 adds the original four-isotone `N=75` doublet systematics and the “central chiral-rotation island plus soft chiral-vibration shores” interpretation. It also supplies the bandhead systematics used by Hecht 2001. The two papers share authors and related `136Pm` evidence, so they form one dependent lineage rather than independent replication; Starosta's printed `136Pm 4n` channel is charge-inconsistent and remains unresolved.

Hecht 2003 extends the direct candidate problem to `N=77` `140Eu` and, unusually for the early corpus, treats chirality and same-configuration triaxial shape coexistence as unresolved co-primary scenarios. Its question-mark title, missing signature partners and explicit lifetime discriminator prevent a confirmed-chirality reading.

Suzuki 2008 adds the first A≈100 lifetime measurement for bands considered chiral doublets in `103,104Rh`. It localizes the reported ratio staggering in `B(E2)` rather than `B(M1)`, but measures only one member of each pair. The absolute-strength comparison between partner bands therefore remains an explicit future test, not a criterion already satisfied.

Luo 2009 extends the candidate map to even-even `108,110,112Ru`. It retains `108Ru` as an unresolved γ-soft control and interprets `110,112Ru` as soft zero-/one-phonon chiral vibrations. The TAC reference remains planar, branching ratios assume pure M1, and the `112Ru` crossing lies beyond harmonic RPA; none of these cases is static chirality established by the source.

Wang 2013 adds a revised `104Ag` A-F coexistence map and an A/B negative-parity candidate. Its local “true chiral partners” wording is stronger than its abstract/summary candidate conclusion, while the evidence remains energy/`S(I)`/ratio/alignment fingerprints without lifetimes, polarization or a dedicated chiral model. The project therefore retains candidate status.

Kuti 2014 adds three proposed pairs in `103Rh`, but its new same-configuration MχD claim applies specifically to negative-parity Bands 3/4 and 6/5. The four-band manifold is experimental; common effective configuration, aplanar geometry and robustness against intrinsic excitation are CDFT/TAC-CDFT/PRM interpretations. Suzuki 2008's positive-pair lifetime member cannot yet be assigned to Kuti Band 1 or 2 by number.

Singh 2016 is a shape-polarization/lifetime control rather than a chiral-doublet source. It supplies independent `131Ce` yrast lifetimes and the complementary low-Ω proton `133Pr` reference, but no partner-band electromagnetic comparison. Its `γ≈−80°/≈0°` contrast is TRS/rotor assisted and must not be counted as direct handed-geometry evidence.

Petrache 2018 adds the first claimed even-even chiral bands and the largest five-pair single-nucleus candidate set at that time. The evidence is sharply nonuniform: D5/D5-chiral has partner-resolved experimental ratios, four weak pairs do not, and explicit 3D aplanar TAC is shown only for D3. The project therefore stores one strengthened pair plus four candidate pairs rather than five equivalent confirmations.

Xiong and Wang 2019 adds a historical data-census layer rather than new experimental evidence. Its 59 reported doublets in 47 nuclei and eight reported/suggested MχD nuclei retain upstream candidate labels and heterogeneous ratio provenance; the project uses it to locate original sources and audit exceptions, not to raise confidence by recounting entries.

Guo 2020 adds a six-band `131Ba` test where pseudospin, chirality and octupole correlations must be separated. D3-D6 are an experimentally established same-parity four-band manifold that cannot be paired uniquely from energy/links; D7/D8 form a negative-parity candidate and a direct E1 network joins the systems. The coupled-symmetry and three-pair reading is retained as RAT-PRM-supported interpretation, not direct geometry.

Wang 2020 adds a review taxonomy: distinct-configuration versus same-configuration MχD, an eight-nucleus experimental-candidate table, a larger predicted-minima table and ideal-PRM linking rules. These are secondary synthesis/model layers. In particular, its `136Nd (5n)` row conflicts with conservation and Petrache's `(4n)` experiment, demonstrating why the review table cannot replace original provenance.

Musangu 2021 adds `104Mo` and revisits `106Mo` as even-even soft chiral-vibration candidates in one extended-TPSM framework. The observed close Bands 4/5 and angular-correlation constraints are separated from the calculated common configuration, connecting E2 strengths and `104Mo` component geometry. Unobserved links, `106Mo` upper-limit tension, fixed-deformation discrepancies and the `γ=100°` axis-order workaround prevent promotion beyond candidate status.

Peng and Chen 2022 contributes a theory-only `102-107Rh` systematics. PC-PK1 3D-TAC CDFT predicts decreasing planar-to-aplanar critical frequencies from `103Rh` to `107Rh`, while `102Rh` remains planar through `0.75 MeV`. This is a mean-field onset map, not a new experimental doublet census: pairing is omitted and partner bands, tunnelling and energy splitting are outside the calculation.

Guo 2022 contributes a complementary theory-only `192-200Tl` screening. PK1 constrained RMF finds multiple favorable triaxial/configuration minima in `192-198Tl` and none in `199,200Tl`; the inferred pair counts reach five in some odd-mass nuclei. These are target counts from static minima, not observed or rotationally calculated doublets, and all Fig.4 experimental information remains secondary.

Xiao 2022 adds an experimental `74As` candidate at the low-`Z` edge of the A≈80 region. Positive-parity Bands 1/2 have a five-link common-configuration network and fitted-TPRM aplanar support; negative-parity Band 3 connects to Band 1 by three E1 transitions. The project retains chirality and octupole correlations as supported interpretations, while preserving the nonideal neutron geometry, missing absolute strengths and explicit contrast with stable-octupole `224Th`.

Wang 2023 adds a secondary map of Chinese experimental studies across A≈130, 100 and 80. Its 23-nucleus census is an original-source routing table, not 23 new confirmations. The strongest reusable lessons are the `106Ag` absolute-strength counterexample, the scarcity of partner-resolved lifetimes, a DSAM correlated-uncertainty warning and a provisional Br-isotope octupole/chirality hypothesis. The printed `82Br/81Kr` `82Ge` targets are charge-inconsistent and remain quarantined pending original-source verification; `82Se` is only a conservation-based likely correction.

Bark 2024 closes the 23-paper sequence with an iThemba programme review. It supplies a restricted-versus-realistic QTR critique of strict fingerprints, reconciles the `81Kr/82Br` targets to `82Se` within the review lineage, and uses `106Ag` plus `193,194Tl` as configuration/pairing counterexamples. Its Eq.(5) polarization asymmetry is algebraically misprinted, and its A≈80/A≈190 nucleus results remain secondary to the cited originals.

## Research Question

- What experimental and theoretical evidence is required to interpret doublet bands as nuclear chiral partners?
- What distinguishes multiple chiral doublet bands from ordinary partner-band or shape-coexistence structures?
- How does triaxial shape coexistence support MχD interpretations?
- What does the `78Br` evidence establish about octupole correlations, and what does it not establish about stable octupole deformation?
- Which commonly used fingerprints remain model dependent?

## Current Hypotheses

- MχD 指认需要对每对双带分别建立实验与模型链，再检验多组三轴形变/组态是否共同支持。
- `133Ce` 的 triaxial shape coexistence 与 `78Br` 的 octupole correlations 可能提供不同的扩展背景，但都不能由近简并能谱单独确定。

## Scope and Limits

核心 reviewed baseline 仍为 [[meng-2010-open-problems-nuclear-chirality]]、Ayangeakaa 2013 与 Liu 2016；当前扩展同时追踪 Guo 2024、Frauendorf 2001、Petrache 1996/1998、Klemme 1999、Hecht/Starosta 2001 等 reviewed/unreviewed 来源的明确证据角色。项目组织理论预期、实验结构、指认、作者解释、模型支持、来源依赖、未解决歧义和未来观测，不声称文献完整覆盖，也不裁决所有候选带的最终性质。

## Model Roles and Limitations

- TAC：角动量取向与 aplanar mean-field solution；总角动量通常不是好量子数，隧穿超出平均场。
- PRM：量子劈裂、跃迁和角动量几何；芯形变、转动惯量和组态通常作为输入。
- constrained RMF/CDFT：形变和组态候选及势能面；模型极小值不是直接实验形变测量。
- `133Ce` TPRM：以 RMF `beta/gamma` 为输入并调整转动惯量；正宇称对另使用 Coriolis attenuation。能量/ratio 趋势再现不是独立实验证明，且未再现实验小幅 staggering。
- `78Br` MDC-CDFT：给出组态形变与 `beta30` softness；TPRM 给出 energies/ratios/effective angles，但忽略的 `f5/2-p3/2` mixing 被作者用于解释 Band 3 偏差。
- `104,106Mo` extended TPSM：固定 `ε/γ`、跨 opposite-parity shells 的 two-quasiparticle basis；可比较 energies/ratios/components，但 generalization 依赖未发表 Ref.39，且 inertia、upper limits 与 `γ=100°` workaround 暴露模型边界。
- `102-107Rh` 3D-TAC CDFT：PC-PK1 self-consistent Routhian/orientation map；固定 one-`h11/2` configuration family 且忽略 pairing，只能给 intrinsic onset 与 yrast observables，不能给 quantum partner bands。
- Bark 2024 restricted/non-restricted QTR：ideal fixed-orbital calculation 可产生 strict degeneracy/staggering rules；realistic multi-orbital space 允许 dominant aplanar geometry 与 sizeable partner differences 共存。该 review-level 结果限制 universal fingerprints，但不直接测量任何核素几何。

## Risks and Blockers

- 常用 fingerprints 缺少 model-independent selection rule；
- 形变、组态与角动量几何主要由模型反演，不能写成直接观测；
- Starosta/Hecht 2001 share evidence provenance; counting them as independent confirmation would inflate confidence. The source-internal `136Pm 4n/p3n` conflict must remain visible.
- `105Ag` contains both a Band-E null and a different D/G candidate; merging them into a single nucleus-level yes/no verdict would destroy the source's evidence structure.
- Suzuki 2008 compares absolute strengths across `103Rh` and `104Rh`, but not between both partners within either nucleus; treating the isotopic similarity as partner equality would invert the paper's main evidence gap.
- Luo 2009's planar TAC plus RPA describes soft orientation motion, not static chirality; extending the harmonic phonon label through the `112Ru` crossing would contradict the paper's explicit breakdown statement.
- Musangu 2021's close Mo bands are experimental, but common configuration, large connecting E2 collectivity and left/right geometry are TPSM outputs; treating those predictions as observations would erase the paper's upper-limit and implementation tensions.
- Peng-Chen 2022 predicts intrinsic aplanar minima but cannot calculate partner bands or tunnelling; recounting its imported Rh data as a fresh experimental confirmation, or extending its one-`h11/2` trend to all MχD configurations, would overstate the result.
- Guo 2022 predicts static Tl minima, not aplanar rotational solutions. Converting each minimum into an observed doublet, resolving `(fp)` to a specific orbital, or using Fig.4 as new experiment would all overstate the source.
- Xiao 2022's aplanar total-I result must retain the neutron three-axis mixing and fitted parameters; its three Band-3 links must not be conflated with the additional low-lying E1, and octupole correlations must not be rewritten as stable deformation.
- Wang 2013's single sentence “true chiral partners” cannot override its candidate abstract/summary and missing absolute/model evidence; promoting the pair would discard the source's own confidence gradient.
- Kuti 2014's “first experimental evidence” is a joint experiment-model statement: removing the common-configuration assignment removes the intrinsic-excitation-robustness conclusion, not the observed four-band manifold.
- Guo 2020's positive quartet is a coupled-symmetry interpretation of one mutually linked four-band manifold; forcing it into two fixed pairs would contradict the paper's own energy/link ambiguity. Direct E1 links support octupole correlations but not stable deformation.
- Wang 2020's observed/predicted tables mix secondary candidate labels with model minima; its `136Nd (5n)` typo and fixed `γ=90°` PRM example make direct metadata/selection-rule reuse unsafe without original sources.
- Wang 2023 and Bark 2024 are overlapping programme reviews, not independent replication. Their `82Ge/82Se` discrepancy is useful correction evidence, while Bark's Eq.(5) shows that even method summaries require formula-level auditing.
- Bark 2024 weakens strict ideal fingerprints but does not abolish evidence requirements; using realistic-mixing arguments to immunize any candidate against conflicting data would be non-falsifiable.
- `78Br` 的八极术语若误写，会把 correlations/softness 错升为 stable deformation；quartet 应写成由共存证据指向的未来观测可能性，不得把当前四条带直接写成已建立 quartet。
