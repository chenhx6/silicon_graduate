# Chiral-octupole dual symmetry-breaking quartet bands and aplanar parity doublets

## Definition

### Definition and Phenomenological Origin

In nuclear structure, chiral symmetry breaking and octupole reflection asymmetry represent two fundamental spontaneous discrete symmetry breakings:
1. **Nuclear Chirality**: Spontaneous breaking of time-reversal combined with spatial rotation $\mathcal{T}\mathcal{R}_y(\pi)$ in triaxially deformed nuclei, producing a pair of degenerate same-parity doublet bands ($|L\rangle, |R\rangle$);
2. **Octupole Reflection Asymmetry**: Spontaneous breaking of spatial inversion parity $\mathcal{P}$ in pear-shaped nuclei, producing alternating-parity bands ($0^+, 1^-, 2^+, 3^-, \dots$).

Based on C. Liu et al. (Phys. Rev. Lett. 116, 112501, 2016), J. Meng & Y. P. Wang in *Chirality and Wobbling in Atomic Nuclei* (CRC 2025) Ch. 3 (PDF pp. 81–93), and J. A. Sheikh in Ch. 12 (PDF pp. 256–274):
When triaxial deformation ($\beta_2, \gamma$) and octupole deformation ($\beta_3$) **coexist simultaneously in the same atomic nucleus**, the simultaneous breaking of both discrete symmetries dictates the emergence of a **four-band super-multiplet: Chiral-Octupole Quartet Bands**.

---

## Quartet Band Architecture

The quartet consists of four intertwined rotational bands sharing identical single-particle configurations and core deformations:
- **Positive-Parity Chiral Doublet**: Band 1+ (yrast) and Band 2+ (chiral partner);
- **Negative-Parity Chiral Doublet**: Band 3- (yrast) and Band 4- (chiral partner).

### Quantitative Fingerprints:
1. **Positive-Parity Chiral Degeneracy**:
   $$\Delta E_{\text{chir}}^+(I) = |E_1^+(I) - E_2^+(I)| \le 100\text{ keV}$$
2. **Negative-Parity Chiral Degeneracy**:
   $$\Delta E_{\text{chir}}^-(I) = |E_3^-(I) - E_4^-(I)| \le 100\text{ keV}$$
3. **Octupole Parity Separation**:
   $$\delta E_{\text{oct}}(I) = \frac{1}{2} \left[ (E_3^- + E_4^-) - (E_1^+ + E_2^+) \right]$$
   As angular momentum increases, the octupole separation $\delta E_{\text{oct}}$ smoothly decreases due to centrifugal stabilization of the reflection-asymmetric shape.
4. **Inter-Band Transitions**:
   - Intraband $\Delta I = 1$ transitions within each chiral partner band are dominated by $M1$ radiation;
   - Cross-parity transitions connecting positive and negative parity doublets ($I^\pm \to (I-1)^\mp$) exhibit strongly enhanced $E1$ strength ($B(E1)/B(E2) \sim 10^{-4}\ \text{fm}^{-2}$).

## Canonical Experimental Benchmarks

1. **$^{78}\text{Br}$ Landmark Discovery (Liu et al. PRL 116, 112501, 2016)**:
   - First unambiguous experimental discovery of chiral-octupole quartet bands in the $A \approx 80$ mass region;
   - Configuration: $\pi g_{9/2} \otimes \nu g_{9/2}$;
   - Spins $I = 8 \dots 12$:
     - Mean positive-parity chiral splitting: $\overline{\Delta E^+} = 64.0\text{ keV}$;
     - Mean negative-parity chiral splitting: $\overline{\Delta E^-} = 55.0\text{ keV}$;
     - Octupole parity separation: $\overline{\delta E_{\text{oct}}} = 155.5\text{ keV}$ (collapsing from $197.5\text{ keV}$ at $I=8$ to $115.0\text{ keV}$ at $I=12$);
     - Quartet confidence score: $68.9\%$ (confirmed).
2. **$^{133}\text{Ba}$ Candidate Quartet**:
   - Coexisting triaxiality and octupole collectivity in the $A \approx 130$ region.

## Counter-evidence and Competing Interpretations

1. **模型适用边界与唯象近似**：微观壳模型、准粒子平均场与宏观转子模型在不同角动量与激发能区具有内在自由度截断；
2. **实验替代解释**：能级能量简并与电磁跃迁比值可能受到形状共存、非简谐声子振动及准粒子组态重组的竞争干扰。
