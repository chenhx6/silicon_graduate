# Radioactivity at the limits of nuclear stability

## Definition

Near proton or neutron drip lines, separation energies, pairing and Coulomb/centrifugal barriers open decay modes that are rare or absent near stability. Decay energies and correlations can then probe shell structure, configuration mixing and continuum dynamics.

## Evidence Boundary

[[pfutzner-2012-radioactive-decays-stability-limits]] is a review map. A nominal `S_p`, `S_n`, `S_{2p}` or `S_{2n}` sign does not alone establish an observed decay mode, exact drip line, configuration or three-body mechanism.

## Mechanism Separation

- β-delayed particles: weak decay populates unbound daughter states; branching depends on β strength and thresholds.
- Single-proton/α radioactivity: barrier penetration and spectroscopic/preformation factors connect energy/half-life to structure.
- True two-proton decay: one-proton emission is energetically closed, requiring a three-body continuum description; sequential and democratic broad-state routes are competing interpretations.
- Neutron/multineutron radioactivity: no Coulomb barrier makes one-neutron radioactivity narrow-window, while 2n/4n phase space can be more favorable.

## Necessary Assumptions

Separate separation-energy signs, observed decay branches, barrier/continuum dynamics and model assignments; a threshold sign alone is not an observed decay mechanism.

## Discriminating Observables

Use half-lives, branching ratios, particle energies, opening angles, relative momenta, γ tags and daughter-state identities to separate sequential, direct and model-supported channels.

## Supporting Evidence

[[pfutzner-2012-radioactive-decays-stability-limits]] supplies the review-level decay map; [[dey-2026-116cs-multifaceted-decay]] supplies a modern multifaceted `116Cs` case with explicit two-proton and cluster-emission boundaries.

## Counter-evidence and Competing Interpretations

Broad unbound states, detector thresholds, sequential decay, β-delayed feeding and low-count correlations can mimic or obscure direct exotic decay.

## Our Current Position

Treat drip-line decay labels as evidence chains combining thresholds, particle correlations and daughter spectroscopy; retain model/low-statistics qualifiers.

## Relevance to This Wiki

The concept reinforces decay-tagging, isomer and configuration audits: measured half-life, branch, energy and correlations must be kept separate from model labels. It is also a cross-domain L3 bridge between shell evolution, pairing, shape coexistence and continuum coupling.
