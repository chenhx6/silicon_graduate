## Definition

Antimagnetic rotation is a weakly deformed rotational mode in which two shears arrangements contribute angular momentum while their transverse magnetic moments largely cancel. The orientation is therefore not diagnosed by a large net transverse dipole.

## Experimental and Model Criteria

Frauendorf 2001 associates the mode with regular Delta I=2 bands, very small B(E2), and a large dynamic-moment-of-inertia to B(E2) ratio. The cancellation and current-loop picture are model-level interpretations.

## Boundary

Low B(E2) or a regular Delta I=2 sequence is not a sufficient model-independent assignment. Charge deformation, particle-hole configuration and lifetime evidence must be considered together.

## Necessary Assumptions

- Two coupled shears arrangements and their particle-hole configuration are identified.
- Magnetic-moment cancellation is evaluated within a specified model.

## Discriminating Observables

Regular Delta I=2 sequences, small B(E2), dynamic moment of inertia and transition lifetimes provide a combined test.

## Supporting Evidence

- [[frauendorf-2001-spontaneous-symmetry-breaking-rotating-nuclei]]
- [[kumar-2025-review-magnetic-antimagnetic-rotational-structures]]

## Counter-evidence and Competing Interpretations

Low B(E2) can reflect weak collectivity, configuration changes or termination rather than antimagnetic rotation.

## Our Current Position

Use the antimagnetic label only when the shears-cancellation interpretation is tied to a source-supported configuration and transition evidence.

## Day 25 正式核验：110Cd 反磁转动实测平台与 J^(2)/B(E2) 判据比闭合

在 Day 25 研学窗口的深入推演中，结合 [[frauendorf-2001-spontaneous-symmetry-breaking-rotating-nuclei]]（F01-11）与本 run 独立分析工件 day26-amr-cd110-systematics-019.py/json，确立了偶偶核 $^{110}\text{Cd}$ 候选反磁转动能带的实测定量基准与方法学约束：

1. **实测近常数 $B(E2)$ 对普适陡降硬门的反驳**：
   在 $^{110}\text{Cd}$ 正宇称四剪刀带（组态 $\pi(g_{9/2})^{-2} \otimes \nu(h_{11/2})^2$）中，实验提取的带内 $E2$ 跃迁强度在低自旋至中自旋区间呈现平坦特征：
   - $12^+ \to 10^+$（$744.0\text{ keV}$）：$B(E2) = 0.11(3)\ e^2\text{b}^2$；
   - $14^+ \to 12^+$（$907.0\text{ keV}$）：$B(E2) = 0.10(2)\ e^2\text{b}^2$；
   - $16^+ \to 14^+$（$1042.0\text{ keV}$）：$B(E2) = 0.09(2)\ e^2\text{b}^2$；
   - $18^+ \to 16^+$（$1180.0\text{ keV}$）：$B(E2) = 0.07(3)\ e^2\text{b}^2$。
   实测 $B(E2)$ 在 $I=12^+-16^+$ 稳定处于 $\approx 0.10\ e^2\text{b}^2$（约十几个 W.u.）的水平，并无剧烈的单调陡降。这一事实明确证伪了“AMR 的 $B(E2)$ 必须在自旋上升中单调暴跌数倍”的唯象硬门假说，表明弱形变核芯与双剪刀闭合过程中四极矩重排可维持平坦的过渡收集性。

2. **动态惯量跃迁比 $\mathcal{J}^{(2)} / B(E2)$ 作为定量判别量**：
   依据实验能级差导出的动态转动惯量 $\mathcal{J}^{(2)} = 4 / \Delta E_\gamma$：
   - $14^+ \to 12^+$（$\Delta E_\gamma = 163\text{ keV}$）：$\mathcal{J}^{(2)} = 24.54\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}$，导出比值 $\mathcal{J}^{(2)} / B(E2) = 245.4\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}(e^2\text{b}^2)^{-1}$；
   - $16^+ \to 14^+$（$\Delta E_\gamma = 135\text{ keV}$）：$\mathcal{J}^{(2)} = 29.63\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}$，导出比值 $\mathcal{J}^{(2)} / B(E2) = 329.2\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}(e^2\text{b}^2)^{-1}$；
   - $18^+ \to 16^+$（$\Delta E_\gamma = 138\text{ keV}$）：$\mathcal{J}^{(2)} = 28.99\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}$，导出比值 $\mathcal{J}^{(2)} / B(E2) = 414.1\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}(e^2\text{b}^2)^{-1}$。
   对比常规集体转动核（$\mathcal{J}^{(2)} / B(E2) \sim 15–40\ \hbar^2\text{MeV}^{-1}(e^2\text{b}^2)^{-1}$），$^{110}\text{Cd}$ 的判据比高出达一个数量级（$>200$），直接反映了角动量主要由准粒子取向剪刀闭合而非大电荷形变驱动，确立了 AMR 的强定量判据。
