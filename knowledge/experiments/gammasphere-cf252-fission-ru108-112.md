# Gammasphere `252Cf` Spontaneous-Fission Ru Experiment

## Identity

High-statistics prompt-fission γ-coincidence dataset used by Luo 2009 to extend negative-parity structures in `108,110,112Ru`.

## Beam, Target and Reaction

A `62 μCi` `252Cf` source produced Ru fragments by spontaneous fission. No beam-target reaction was used.

## Detector Configuration

Gammasphere recorded prompt γ rays. The source paper does not restate a detector count or facility run location, so those details are not inferred.

## Trigger and Coincidence Conditions

The dataset contains `5.7×10^11` triple-and-higher-fold coincidences. Subdivision by detector angle enabled γ-γ angular correlations.

## Data Products

- weak extended negative-parity `ΔI=1` structures and interband links;
- selected angular-correlation coefficients/spin and dipole assignments;
- same-spin separation, `S(I)` and double-gated branching ratios.

## Nuclei and Bands Studied

[[108ru-negative-parity-doublet-structures]], [[110ru-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]] and [[112ru-chiral-vibration-doublet-candidate]].

## Known Limitations

Branching-derived ratios assume pure M1 for `ΔI=1` transitions. Lifetimes are not measured beyond the reported `<1 ns` bandhead limits. Weak interband transitions are often upper limits.
