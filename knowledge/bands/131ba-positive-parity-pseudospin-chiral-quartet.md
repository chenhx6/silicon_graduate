# `131Ba` D3-D6 Positive-Parity Quartet

## Stable Identity

Guo 2020 D3-D6 are four mutually linked positive-parity dipole bands. D4 is newly established; D3, D5 and D6 are extended. Because D4-D6 exchange energy ordering and every two bands are linked, the stable object is the four-band manifold, not two source-independent fixed pairs.

## Level and Transition Data

Figure 1 gives the D3-D6 energies, intensities and dense interband network. Many M1/E2 links and a smaller number of weak E2 links connect the four bands; source-specific numerical values remain on the source/raw level scheme rather than being duplicated here.

## Spin-Parity Evidence

Coincidence links, `R_ac`, observed ΔI=1/2 links and four-angle angular distributions constrain the common positive parity and spin sequence. Several high-spin labels remain uncertain in Fig.1.

## Configuration Assignment

Systematics, signature behavior and `8-10ℏ` alignments support the `πh11/2(g7/2,d5/2)⊗νh11/2` family. RAT-PRM gives dominant `g7/2` character for D3/D4 and stronger mixed `d5/2` components for D5/D6; this is a model decomposition, not direct orbital measurement.

## Proposed Interpretation

The paper interprets the manifold as the first pseudospin-chiral quartet: pseudospin and chiral splittings are comparable enough that energies and links do not divide the bands cleanly into two pairs.

## Discriminating Observables

Partner-resolved lifetimes/absolute strengths, configuration-sensitive moments and the deferred RAT-PRM azimuthal/angular-momentum plots are required to test the coupled-symmetry assignment.

## Competing Interpretations

Strong four-state configuration mixing, ordinary signature structures or two differently coupled band pairs may reproduce parts of the manifold. The calculated relative E1 strengths feeding D5 are significantly below experiment.
