## 1. Core Frameworks and Theoretical Invariants

### 1.1 Pairing Correlations and the BCS Ground State
- Short-range attractive nuclear force between identical nucleons in time-reversed orbits $(j m, j -m)$:
  $$H_{\text{pair}} = - G \sum_{m, m' > 0} a_{j m}^\dagger a_{j -m}^\dagger a_{j -m'} a_{j m'}.$$
- BCS pairing gap equation:
  $$\Delta = G \sum_{\nu > 0} u_\nu v_\nu = \frac{G}{2} \sum_{\nu > 0} \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{(\epsilon_\nu - \lambda)^2 + \Delta^2}}.$$
- Quasiparticle excitation energy:
  $$E_\nu = \sqrt{(\epsilon_\nu - \lambda)^2 + \Delta^2}.$$
  The odd-even mass difference yields the experimental gap: $\Delta \a12 A^{-1/2}\text{ MeV}$.

### 1.2 Seniority Scheme and Fractional Parentage
- Seniority quantum number $v$: number of unpaired particles.
- In a single-$j$ shell, the seniority ground state of an even-even nucleus has $v=0, J=0^+$.
- Electromagnetic transition selection rules:
  - Odd-multipole electric operators ($E1, E3$) and even-multipole magnetic operators ($M2$) conserve seniority ($\Delta v = 0$);
  - Even-multipole electric operators ($E2$) change seniority by $\Delta v = \pm 2$ in the half-filled shell ($n = (2j+1)/2$), leading to the vanishing of the diagonal quadrupole moment $Q \equiv 0$ and isomerism.

## 2. Experimental Diagnostic Rules

1. Odd-even mass staggering evaluated via three-point or four-point finite difference formulas isolates the pairing gap $\Delta_n$ or $\Delta_p$.
2. Two-nucleon transfer reactions $(p, t)$ and $(t, p)$ on superfluid nuclei exhibit strong ground-state cross-section enhancement ($\propto (N_{\text{pairs}})^2$), acting as a direct probe of nuclear Josephson pairing vibrations.
