# Glossary

**Alaga rules** — rotational intensity relations for transitions between bands with specified intrinsic K values (Ch. 6).
**ARC** — average resonance capture; neutron-capture spectroscopy used to constrain level completeness and spin/parity patterns (Ch. 10).
**Coriolis coupling** — rotation-induced coupling between intrinsic states, often changing K by one unit (Ch. 8).
**Casten triangle** — IBM map of U(5), SU(3), and O(6) dynamical-symmetry limits (Ch. 6).
**Decoupled band** — odd-particle rotational sequence whose energies approach those of the even-even core after rotation alignment (Ch. 8).
**Davydov-Filippov model** — fixed-gamma asymmetric rotor model (Ch. 6).
**Gamma softness** — collective wave-function freedom along the gamma coordinate; it is distinct from fixed triaxiality (Ch. 6).
**Level repulsion** — avoided crossing produced by off-diagonal mixing; the minimum two-state separation is `2|V|` (Ch. 1).
**m-scheme** — construction of allowed multiparticle angular momenta from magnetic-substate occupations (Ch. 5).
**NpNn scheme** — valence proton-neutron product systematics for collectivity (Ch. 6).
**P-factor** — `P = Np Nn/(Np+Nn)`, a scaled collectivity variable (Ch. 6).
**Seniority** — number of unpaired particles in a single-j configuration (Ch. 5).
**Stephens rotation alignment** — high-j quasiparticle angular-momentum alignment with the rotational axis at increasing spin (Ch. 8).
**Two-state mixing** — diagonalization of two coupled configurations; use it to estimate energy shifts, amplitudes, and transition cancellations (Ch. 1).
**Wilets-Jean model** — gamma-unstable collective limit, contrasted with fixed-gamma rotation (Ch. 6).
