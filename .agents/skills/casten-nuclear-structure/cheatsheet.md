# Decision Cheatsheet

| If you observe | First test | Do not conclude alone |
|---|---|---|
| avoided crossing | two-state `Delta E >= 2|V|` and mixing amplitudes | a unique microscopic configuration |
| weak interband E2 | phase-aware amplitude expansion and `Delta Q` | absence of configuration mixing |
| gamma-band staggering | several consecutive levels with an explicit S(J) convention | a unique gamma angle or rigid triaxiality |
| large `NpNn` or P | compare E2 strengths and moments | a direct deformation measurement |
| high-spin alignment/backbend | Nilsson orbit, Fermi surface, pairing and Coriolis matrix element | a universal band-crossing threshold |
| strong Coulomb-excitation yield | p, Z, energy, nuclear-contact and multistep paths | a direct B(E2) without feeding/model audit |
| many ARC primary transitions | allowed spin/parity and completeness logic | complete spectroscopy outside the populated window |
| high-spin gamma cascades | fusion-evaporation entry spin and decay-out/side-feeding | a unique band assignment from energies alone |

**Mode labels**: TW/LW, chirality, signature partner and gamma softness require model assumptions plus electromagnetic observables. Use Casten's cancellation argument as a counterexample whenever a simple “mixing implies strong link” inference appears.
