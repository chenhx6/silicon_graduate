# Chapter 8: Nilsson Applications and Coriolis Refinements

## Core Idea
Rotation couples intrinsic Nilsson states. The resulting K mixing, decoupling and high-spin rotation alignment explain transfer cross sections, signature patterns, backbending and decoupled bands.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **K=1/2 decoupling**: use the decoupling parameter `a` to relate odd-A energies to the even-even core pattern.
- **Coriolis mixing**: include off-diagonal terms between states differing in K by approximately one unit; empirical attenuation may be needed but must be reported as model input.
- **Stephens rotation alignment**: high-j unique-parity orbitals align with the rotational axis as spin increases, producing decoupled sequences and crossings.

## Key Concepts
Coriolis operator; K mixing; decoupling parameter; unique-parity orbit; rotation alignment; crossing frequency; backbending; transfer spectroscopic strength.

A decoupled-band pattern is configuration and inertia dependent. It cannot by itself establish triaxiality, wobbling or a unique band assignment.
