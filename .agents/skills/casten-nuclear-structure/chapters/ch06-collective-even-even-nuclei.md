# Chapter 6: Collective Excitations in Even-Even Nuclei

## Core Idea
Casten connects spherical vibrations, axial rotation, fixed triaxiality, gamma softness, IBM dynamical symmetries and microscopic valence-nucleon trends into one collectivity narrative.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Gamma-rigid versus gamma-soft comparison**: use consecutive gamma-band staggering and energy ratios, not one fitted gamma value.
- **Casten triangle**: use U(5), SU(3), O(6) as limiting reference points for spherical, axially deformed and gamma-unstable behavior.
- **NpNn and P systematics**: compare valence proton-neutron collectivity across a shell; retain saturation and intruder-state caveats.

## Key Concepts
R4/2; B(E2); gamma band; Davydov-Filippov; Wilets-Jean; IBM; U(5); SU(3); O(6); S(J); NpNn; P-factor.

S(J), R4/2 and NpNn are diagnostic systematics, not unique shape observables. Odd-A high-spin bands require separate Coriolis and configuration analysis.
