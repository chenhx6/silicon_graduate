# Chapter 5: Multiparticle Configurations

## Core Idea
The m-scheme, coefficients of fractional parentage and seniority organize many-particle states while exposing how pairing controls spectra, `B(E2)`, quadrupole moments and quasiparticle thresholds.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **m-scheme counting**: enumerate allowed `J` values from magnetic substates before invoking full recoupling algebra.
- **Seniority scheme**: track paired versus unpaired particles in a single-`j` shell.
- **Pairing-correlation picture**: use odd-even mass trends, ground-state spin, quasiparticle thresholds and moment-of-inertia changes as connected manifestations, with model boundaries explicit.

## Key Concepts
m-scheme; CFP; seniority; `j^n` configuration; pair gap; quasiparticle; particle-hole symmetry; fractional filling; ARC completeness.

## Worked Example
For a `j^n` configuration, seniority `v` counts particles not coupled into `J=0` pairs. Casten gives

`B(E2; 2+_1 -> 0+_1) proportional to n(1 - n/(2j+1)) ~= f(1-f)`, with `f=n/(2j+1)`, which peaks near mid-shell. A seniority-preserving even-tensor matrix element contains `(2j+1-2n)/(2j+1-2v)`, so a quadrupole moment vanishes and changes sign at mid-shell. These are different channels and must not be merged into one “collectivity” label.

Pair scattering across the Fermi surface gives `E_i = sqrt((epsilon_i-lambda)^2 + Delta^2)`, an even-nucleus two-quasiparticle threshold near `2 Delta`, and a compressed odd-nucleus spectrum. ARC level-density growth offers an experimental completeness-based view of the gap.

## Connects To
The seniority and pairing limits help identify when a high-spin band is dominated by single-particle alignment rather than a collective phonon. They also set the baseline for blocked-BCS, Coriolis anti-pairing and A≈130 quasiparticle calculations.

## Boundary
The seniority formulas require a near single-`j`, seniority-conserving space. Core polarization, multi-`j` mixing, `T=0` proton-neutron correlations, deformation, Coriolis anti-pairing and blocking produce residual terms. ARC completeness is window- and selection-rule-dependent.
