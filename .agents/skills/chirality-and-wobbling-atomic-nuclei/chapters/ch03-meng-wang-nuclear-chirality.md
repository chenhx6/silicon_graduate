## 1. Relativistic Mean-Field and Tilted Axis Cranking (TAC)

Meng and Wang present the self-consistent microscopic theory of nuclear chirality based on Covariant Density Functional Theory (CDFT) and 3D Tilted Axis Cranking (3D-TAC).

### 1.1 The Chiral Triad and Aplanar Geometry
- In a triaxially deformed nucleus ($\beta_2 \a0.2 - 0.3, \gamma \a30^\circ$), the Dirac equation is solved in the rotating frame with cranking vector $\vec{\omega} = (\omega_x, \omega_y, \omega_z)$:
  $$\left[ \vec{\alpha}\cdot(\vec{p} - \vec{V}) + \beta(M + S) + V - \vec{\omega}\cdot\vec{J} \right] \psi_i = \epsilon_i \psi_i.$$
- When a valence proton particle occupies the bottom of the $h_{11/2}$ shell (orbitals $[550]1/2^-, [541]3/2^-$), its orbital density concentrates in the $y$-$z$ plane, minimizing energy by aligning $\vec{j}_p$ along the short axis ($x$);
- When a valence neutron hole occupies the top of the $h_{11/2}$ shell (orbitals $[505]11/2^-, [514]9/2^-$), its orbital density concentrates in the $x$-$y$ plane, minimizing energy by aligning $\vec{j}_n$ along the long axis ($z$);
- The collective triaxial core possesses the largest moment of inertia along the intermediate axis ($y$), aligning $\vec{R}_{\text{core}}$ along $y$;
- The resulting triad forms an aplanar vector configuration with non-zero scalar triple product:
  $$\vec{J}_{\text{tot}} \cdot (\vec{j}_p \times \vec{j}_n) \neq 0.$$

### 1.2 Spontaneous Chiral Symmetry Breaking and Restoration
- In the body-fixed intrinsic frame, the right-handed state $|R\rangle$ and left-handed state $|L\rangle$ are degenerate in energy but cannot be connected by any continuous 3D spatial rotation;
- The discrete time-reversal-rotational symmetry $\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{T} \mathcal{R}_y(\pi)$ is spontaneously broken;
- In the laboratory frame, quantum instanton tunneling through the chiral barrier restores the symmetry, forming parity-adapted symmetric and antisymmetric eigenstates:
  $$|+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (|R\rangle + |L\rangle), \quad |-\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (|R\rangle - |L\rangle).$$
  These states manifest experimentally as chiral doublet bands with energy splitting $\Delta E(I) = 2 \hbar \Omega \exp(-S_{\text{inst}}/\hbar)$.

## 2. Multiple Chiral Doublets ($M\chi D$) Classification

Meng and Wang formulate the comprehensive categorization of $M\chi D$:
1. **Type I ($M\chi D$ with Different Quasiparticle Configurations)**:
   - Predicted by Peng et al. (PRC 2008) and experimentally verified in $^{133}\text{Ce}$ (Ayangeakaa et al. PRL 2013);
   - Different combinations of valence nucleons (e.g. 1-quasiparticle vs 3-quasiparticle, or positive-parity vs negative-parity configurations) each form a stable aplanar geometry.
2. **Type II ($M\chi D$ with Shape Coexistence)**:
   - Same quasiparticle configuration occupying distinct triaxial minima on the constrained CDFT potential energy surface ($\gamma \approx 20^\circ$ prolate-triaxial vs $\gamma \approx -40^\circ$ oblate-triaxial).
3. **Type III ($M\chi D$ with Chiral Phonons)**:
   - Same quasiparticle configuration and deformation, representing ground-state and multi-phonon chiral vibrational tunneling excitations across the instanton barrier (e.g. $^{103}\text{Rh}$, Kuti et al. PRL 2014).
