# PDM 0.1.1 independent review

Line references are relative to `raw/code/PDM/GUI/`. Findings apply to the 0.1.1 source inspected on November 20, 2026.

## Findings

1. **P1: Complementary point groups can produce a posterior from roundoff.** `src/multipolepdm/physics/symmetries.py:31` folds complementary polar angles only for ADO or averaged coincidences. Point-selected singleton groups bypass the legacy exact unit-ratio identity at `src/multipolepdm/physics/symmetries.py:78`. With the packaged P_DCO and PDCO examples, set A to `(49 deg, 0 deg)`, B to `(131 deg, 37 deg)`, the polarization target to `75 deg`, and the PDCO polarization gate to `(51 deg, 88 deg)`. On a 51 by 31 scan with angle bounds `[-1.4, 1.4]` rad, the scalar definition gives `R_DCO == 1` at all 1,581 nodes and rejects the zero Jacobian. For the equivalent singleton groups with `select_azimuth=True`, `max(abs(R_DCO - 1))` is `3.55e-15`. `map_density` accepts and normalizes both grids, with Jacobian maxima of `3.11e-15` and `3.34e-15`, respectively. This yields numerical interval estimates for an unidentifiable two-parameter scan. A regression should assert the exact identity and mapping rejection for this grouped geometry in both modes.

2. **P2: A figure export can freeze the GUI when another result becomes ready.** `src/multipolepdm/gui/results.py:54` builds the preview on the Qt thread. `src/multipolepdm/plotting/overview.py:202` takes the same lock that `src/multipolepdm/plotting/export.py:48` holds throughout `savefig`. Offscreen, using the real single-panel PDF export and ready-preview paths, a 1.5 s injected delay inside `savefig` increased the second `show_result` call from 0.42 s to 2.67 s. A separate run with a 10 ms Qt heartbeat recorded a 2.87 s gap while a save with a 1.25 s injected delay overlapped a ready preview. The export and second preview eventually completed. The existing slow-data-writer scenario exercises a different path and misses this lock contention.

3. **P2: Manual PDCO cannot set the independent azimuth selections in plan section 2.6.** `src/multipolepdm/gui/window.py:110` provides one shared checkbox. The Advanced section at `src/multipolepdm/gui/window.py:164` has no independent controls, and `read_job` at `src/multipolepdm/gui/window.py:350` leaves both overrides at their defaults. The JSON route preserves and applies them. On the packaged PDCO example at `(angle, sigma) = (0.15 rad, 1.2)`, averaging the polarization-gate azimuth alone changed P from `-0.757159` to `-0.541421` while holding R at `0.692302`. Averaging the DCO azimuth alone changed R to `0.682181` while holding P fixed. A manual user cannot express either geometry.

4. **P3: A width-unit switch can silently change the grid.** `src/multipolepdm/gui/editors.py:122` sets one positive widget minimum for both coordinate systems. `src/multipolepdm/gui/editors.py:155` writes converted values into those same limits. With reference spin `0.5`, setting `sigma/I` min/max/step to `1e-10 / 1e-8 / 1.5e-10` makes a 67-node width axis. Switching to `sigma` clamps the min and step from `5e-11` and `7.5e-11` to `1e-10` each, yielding 50 nodes. These values pass grid validation on both sides of the toggle.

## P1 and P3 fix verification

- **P1 resolved.** The new shortcut at `src/multipolepdm/physics/symmetries.py:58` requires a single exact folded polar angle across all positive-weight rows; it leaves the point azimuths in the response calculation. For the aligned axial source, each unpolarized detector direction is antipodally equivalent. After placing both directions on the same cone, reflection in their azimuthal bisector exchanges them while preserving the source and the roles of the target and gate in the cascade. A separate 9j coefficient calculation checked 54 point pairs across both target emission orders, three mixing ratios, mixed gates, and an unobserved intermediate. The largest deviation from one was `4.44e-16`. An off-cone pair gave `R_DCO = 0.986450`, and a one-ULP shift off the cone withheld the production shortcut. An off-cone zero-weight row did not affect identity detection. The new regressions also confirm exact unit ratios and rejection of the degenerate map in both DCO modes.
- **P3 verified at fixed spin.** At `I = 0.5`, the original 67-node width axis survived switching from `sigma/I` to `sigma` and back with identical internal coordinates.
- **Historical P3 follow-up, fixed.** On the earlier revision, switching units used the previous widget limits. Four cycles of selecting `sigma` at `I = 2` and `sigma/I` at `I = 40` shrank the normalized upper limit to `0.0625`. Returning to `I = 2` clamped both internal bounds to `0.125` and caused `read_job` to reject the grid. See the final verification for the current behavior.
- Independently reran `test_review_regressions.py`, `test_detector_groups.py`, `test_azimuth_average.py`, `test_step_grid.py`, and `test_independent_reference.py` with Qt offscreen. All 78 tests passed in 16.25 s. The two P2 findings were outside this follow-up.

## Final disposition

- **P1 resolved.** Complementary point-selected groups produce an exact unit ratio and reject degenerate mapping in both DCO modes. The 54 independent coefficient checks and off-cone controls above support the scope of the shortcut.
- **P3 resolved.** `src/multipolepdm/gui/editors.py:159` rebases the limits on each switch. At fixed `I = 0.5`, both axes were identical after switching in either direction, with 67 width nodes. Six spin and unit cycles kept the normalized upper limit at `10000`. At `I = 2`, restoring the displayed `0.005 sigma/I` step produced an accepted `1799 x 186` grid with internal sigma bounds `[0.15, 2.0]` and step `0.01`. The previously reported node cap follows from the intended policy of retaining the displayed step when the spin changes. That follow-up is withdrawn.
- **P2 manual azimuth resolved.** The PDCO controls independently select azimuth for the DCO ratio and the polarization gate. All four combinations passed their offscreen checks through manual input, JSON round trip, and response evaluation. `src/multipolepdm/gui/window.py:246`, `tests/test_gui_averaging.py:64`
- **P2 new-preview freeze resolved.** `MainWindow` defers a ready preview when `ResultsWindow.try_create` cannot acquire the Matplotlib lock immediately. The real single-panel export overlap passed again offscreen. `src/multipolepdm/gui/window.py:501`, `src/multipolepdm/gui/results.py:40`, `tests/test_app_regressions.py:296`

Two related P2 cases remained open at that checkpoint. Both were reproduced independently. The frozen repair and verification are recorded below.

- A fourth ready snapshot evicts the oldest pending preview at `src/multipolepdm/gui/window.py:503`, even if the oldest save failed. During a held figure export, one failed save followed by three successful saves left only the three successful snapshots pending. Selecting the failed task after the export returned `This task has no saved result to open.` The failed job's retryable snapshot had been discarded.
- **Restore full ranges** on an existing preview schedules a Qt canvas redraw at `src/multipolepdm/gui/results.py:151`. That redraw can still wait on Matplotlib's shared render lock. With a 1.5 s delay injected inside the real background renderer, the maximum Qt heartbeat gap increased from 0.11 s to 1.84 s.

The bounded closeout rerun passed five tests in 8.50 s. The worker regression log records 21 passing GUI tests and `full-regression-reviewed.log` records 215 passing tests. Those runs predate the repairs for the two cases above.

## Final P2 repair verification

- **Failed-save snapshot eviction resolved.** The original preview can still be evicted from the GUI cache. The job manager now retains the frozen result and summary against the job's existing task and result reservations. In the offscreen regression, a failed save followed by four successful jobs while an export held the lock was evicted from the preview cache. **Open selected result** recovered the same snapshot and **Retry data save** wrote it to a healthy directory without recalculation. The saved density matched the retained density exactly. `src/multipolepdm/jobs/manager.py:93`, `tests/test_app_regressions.py:511`
- **Existing-preview redraw freeze resolved.** `DeferredFigureCanvas.draw` acquires the shared Matplotlib lock without blocking. A single child timer retries after contention, and close stops the timer. The offscreen regressions exercised restore, resize, and toolbar redraw while a real PDF export held Matplotlib's render lock. Qt stayed responsive, the redraw completed after export, and closing a pending canvas prevented a late redraw. The worker recorded maximum heartbeat gaps of approximately 11 ms. `src/multipolepdm/gui/results.py:47`, `tests/test_app_regressions.py:429`
- **Resource balance verified.** Retention keeps the existing reservations and retry transfers them to the writer. A terminal save or cancellation releases them, and shutdown drains retained snapshots before waiting on workers. The manager tests covered full-queue retry, retry submission rollback, repeated failures, cancellation, and shutdown. No reservation leak or double release was found in these paths. `src/multipolepdm/jobs/manager.py:143`, `src/multipolepdm/jobs/manager.py:212`, `tests/test_jobs.py`

The independent offscreen rerun passed all 13 tests in 19.63 s. Both follow-up P2 findings are closed. No findings from this independent review remain open.
