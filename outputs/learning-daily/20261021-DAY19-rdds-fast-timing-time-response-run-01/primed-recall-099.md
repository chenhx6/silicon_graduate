4. Rare-earth / deformed region (A ~ 160-180): 176W, 172Hf, 152Gd/Sm. Fast timing with LaBr3/XP20D0 (MSCD and slope methods) covering 10 ps to 1.5 ns; active BGO background suppression; PRD walk calibration with 152Eu.
