# Active recall before direct PartII body

Frozen before reading the electromagnetic PartII body. This is primed by the just-read CH16 paper, not a blind/scored examination.

CH16 quantizes theta/phi orientations at each fixed frequency, uses sqrt(detB)dtheta dphi, four reflection sectors and periodic boundaries; its spin is a probability-averaged TAC magnitude with −1/2 correction, not exact angular-momentum projection. It gives spectra/model benchmarking but no coherent experimental band-to-band electromagnetic matrix-element set. A possible next prescription is to average local TAC B values using the collective density, but that is a hypothesis to check, not an established PartII method.

Questions: Is PartII B an average of a local *rate* or a squared coherent collective transition amplitude? Which wavefunction(s)/I mapping/sector(s) occur? Can two states with opposite chiral/signature reflections have different E2/M1 selection rules in this construction? Are interband transitions actually computed? What are the moment/g factors, mass prescription and boundary choices? Does PRM agreement survive at low/high spins, and which parameters are chosen rather than predicted?
