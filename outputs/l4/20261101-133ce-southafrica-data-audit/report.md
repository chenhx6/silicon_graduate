# `133Ce` South Africa data audit and L4 readiness

## Scope

This is the first L4 data audit for the user-supplied South Africa dataset.
The user-authorized cleanup preserves file hashes for moved material, records
all destinations, archives named backup drawings, and removes only
bitwise-identical redundant utility copies. The requested semantic partition is
represented by the top-level directories:

- `raw/data/SouthAfrica/` is the experiment-location root.
- `raw/data/SouthAfrica/133Ce_iThemba2019/` is the immutable acquisition and
  processing layer: experiment records, detector setup, calibration, sorting
  configuration, calibration source spectra, cube, supplied ROOT
  representations and Radware/GASP matrix products.
- `raw/data/SouthAfrica/133Ce/` is the derived-analysis and systematics layer:
  analysis spreadsheets, figures, code, notebooks and cross-nucleus comparisons.

## Inventory baseline

The pre-cleanup hash inventory found `2,906` files and `6,221,719,622` bytes;
there were no zero-byte files. After the authorized cleanup, the archive has
`2,902` files and `6,220,928,509` bytes. The four removed files total
`791,113` bytes and were exact byte-for-byte duplicates of retained shared
copies. The two main layers originally contained `118` files in
`133Ce_iThemba2019` and `2,788` files in `133Ce`; their current counts include
the new subdirectories and the derived-product moves. Exact content duplicates
occurred only in three small calibration/program groups:

1. `asc2netEGASPware.C` appears in two calibration subtrees;
2. `Mxa100.ini` appears in Ba and Eu calibration subtrees;
3. `mxa.exe` appears in Ba and Eu calibration subtrees.

The shared canonical copies are now in
`raw/data/SouthAfrica/133Ce/calibration/_shared/`; the duplicate context copies
were removed under the user's cleanup authorization. The cleanup manifest
retains each pre-change hash and destination.

## L4 input closure

The acquisition layer contains a complete-looking processing chain:

- `config/PR295offline` and `config/PR295offline75_95.dat` preserve the clover/LEPS/LaBr
  channel layout, gain arrays and known sorter problems;
- `cube/` contains `ce.cub`, `ce.scr`, `ce.2dp`, `ce.spe`, `ce.lev`,
  `ener.cal`, `mapfile.dat` and the sort source/readme;
- `mat/` contains `gg.cmat`, `dco.cmat`, `h/l/s/v.cmat` and Radware matrix
  products;
- `133Ce - calibration/` contains PDCO, ADO and energy calibration workbooks;
- `analysis/intensity_iThemba2019_export.xlsx` contains intensity, ADO, DCO, asymmetry, mixing-ratio,
  branching-ratio and calibration sheets.

The analysis workbook exposes concrete count-level inputs and formulas. The
audit script recomputes the ADO, DCO and asymmetry formula families from the
stored counts and calibration factors and compares them with cached workbook
values. The machine-readable result is
`outputs/l4/20261101-133ce-southafrica-data-audit/workbook-audit.json`,
generated by `audit_133ce_workbook.py`.

The selected ROOT matrices were also opened with
`inspect_133ce_root.C`. `133Ce_iThemba2019/mat/root/g45allmatprompt.root`,
`g90allmatprompt.root`,
`gg.root`, `hgg.root` and `vgg.root` are readable `TH2I` objects with
`4096×4096` bins, channel ranges `[0,2048]` on both axes and approximately
`1.67772×10^7` entries each. This verifies file structure and readability,
not event provenance or response covariance.

A bin-by-bin integer comparison between the five ROOT histograms and candidate
`.m4b` files was completed in `matrix-content-audit.json`. It reveals an
important provenance division across the supplied representations:

1. `g45allmatprompt.root` and `g90allmatprompt.root` match
   `m4b/g45allmatprompt.m4b` and `m4b/g90allmatprompt.m4b` with zero bin
   differences across all `16,777,216` bins.
2. `gg.root`, `hgg.root` and `vgg.root` do not match `ggmatprompt.m4b`,
   `hggmatprompt.m4b` or `vggmatprompt.m4b`; their sums differ by up to
   `28%` to `39%`.
3. Each of `gg.root`, `hgg.root` and `vgg.root` instead reproduces
   `m4b/ggmat.m4b`, `m4b/hggmat.m4b` and `m4b/vggmat.m4b` with zero bin
   differences.

This establishes that the supplied ROOT matrices are export representations of
two different processing branches: one with an explicit prompt selection, and
one without. They cannot be used as an interchangeable joint coincidence
dataset until the gate and timing cuts are documented.

## Current result boundary

This run reaches **partial L4 readiness**. It can perform reproducible
workbook arithmetic and response-contract checks. It cannot yet claim a
publication-grade event-level reanalysis because the current audit has not
closed:

- producer/version identity for every matrix and workbook export;
- ROOT/cube event-to-matrix provenance and gate history;
- detector-response and efficiency covariance across ADO, DCO, PDCO and
  asymmetry sheets;
- background/random/sideband treatment and independent holdout definition;
- formula-version and calibration-version linkage between `133Ce` results and
  `133Ce_iThemba2019` inputs.

The existing derived sheets are therefore analysis results and candidate
inputs, not independent experimental measurements. Any 133Ce wobbling,
chirality, mixing-ratio or cross-nucleus systematic conclusion remains
provisional until these lineage and covariance gates are closed.

5. Only after those steps run a count-level likelihood or Geant4 response
   simulation; no synthetic counts or posterior is created by this report.
