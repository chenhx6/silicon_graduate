**The current assignment conclusion changes:** Vogt et al. (2017) supplies a tentative \(^{137}\)Ba \((15/2^-)\) state at **2229.8 keV**, feeding \(11/2^-\) at **661.7 keV**. The earlier absence of an assigned \(15/2\) level remains a historical finding about the checked 2007 ENSDF objects. It is no longer the current literature conclusion.

The critical timing distinction is explicit in Section III B: gating on the **downstream 1568-keV photon** measures the decay of the **upstream 2349.9-keV isomer**. Six fit windows between 100 and 700 ns produce the quoted 589(20)-ns half-life. It cannot be assigned to the 2229.8-keV state. The statement about finding no further few-ns lifetimes applies to states **above 2349.9 keV**, not a quantified limit on the lower target state.

Table II leaves both the 120.1- and 1568.2-keV \(I_\gamma\) cells blank; its normalized intensity of 100 belongs to the 274.5-keV transition feeding the isomer. No target ICC is numerically reported. Table III says its \(B(E2)\) values have already been corrected with BrIcc, reference [69]. Do not assume a missing ICC is zero or apply a second correction to the published strength.

The \(19/2\rightarrow15/2\) measurement cannot replace the required \(15/2\rightarrow11/2\) strength. It also does not directly enter the initial-\(11/2\) cubic moment: in \(R_{ia}R_{ab}R_{bi}\), both intermediate states must connect to \(i=11/2\) through rank 2, allowing only \(J_a,J_b=7/2,\ldots,15/2\). A \(19/2\) state fails that outer-leg triangle condition. It remains a useful **separate** E2 benchmark, with state mapping fixed before comparison. The paper discusses possible exchange of two calculated \(19/2\) states; that is not permission to select an IBFM ordinal after seeing the strength.

Table III's **135Xe** result, 9.0(9) ns and 0.52(6) W.u., is another isotope and transition; it must not be attached to **135Ba**. The quoted 133Te result is previous work and is used to adjust SN100PN's proton effective charge in this paper, so that model agreement is calibration-linked. No additional nuclear search or model fit was performed here.
