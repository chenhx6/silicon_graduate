# L3: direct E3 versus E1 and shape labels in neutron-rich Ba

## Question

Does a small E1 dipole moment imply weak octupole collectivity in neighboring Ba isotopes?

## Evidence

- `144Ba`: Bucher 2016 direct Coulomb excitation gives `B(E3;3−→0+)=48^{+25}_{−34} W.u.` (PDF p.4, Table I).
- `146Ba`: external Bucher 2017 measurement gives `B(E3;3−→0+)=48^{+21}_{−29} W.u.` despite an order-of-magnitude E1 dipole-moment reduction; GOSIA Table I, PDF pp.2–3.
- The 2017 SCCM/GCM model associates the E1 cancellation with neutron orbital occupancy while retaining strong octupole strength (PDF pp.3–5, Figs.3–5). The model is a mechanism proposal, not an independent measurement.

## Decision

The direct E3 measurements show that E1 magnitude is not a one-to-one proxy for octupole collectivity in this isotope pair. The evidence ladder ranks direct E3 above relative E1 links and above intrinsic PES minima. The inference of a static `β3` remains model dependent.

## Reverse test

A high-statistics E3 determination in `148Ba`, together with a common E1/E2 matrix-element fit and microscopic occupancy calculation across `144–148Ba`, could test whether E1 variation follows the proposed neutron-orbital cancellation. A contrary E3 trend would revise the present isotope-systematics conclusion.

## L4 readiness and stop

No public event matrices, full GOSIA covariance or SCCM code are supplied. The L3 comparison is complete and stops at source-level uncertainties; no L4 refit is claimed.
