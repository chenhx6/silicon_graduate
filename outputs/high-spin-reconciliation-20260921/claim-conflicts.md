# High-spin cross-source claim conflicts

This register records disagreements that must remain visible in synthesis and paper-facing use. A conflict is not resolved by choosing the newest or most convenient value; each row retains scope, locator and evidence type.

## Resolution policy

- Keep both source claims and their locators in synthesis pages.
- Label each comparison `supports`, `limits`, `revises`, `conflicts` or `no material change`.
- Do not clear `needs_review` solely because two papers agree on a headline number.
- Promote a conflict to L3 only when a discriminating observable or model comparison is specified.
